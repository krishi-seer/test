## Key Intelligence Pillars

### Elite Document Scanner (GPT-4o)
Our "Doc Scanner" uses **GPT-4o Vision** with specialized agricultural OCR to read **Soil Health Cards** in local scripts (Hindi/Odia/English). It extracts 12+ nutrient metrics (NPK, pH, OC) instantly and provides precision fertilizer advice, removing the need for technical expertise.

### Autonomous Scheme Engine (Self-Healing)
A proprietary background engine that maintains a comprehensive database of **50+ Central and State agricultural schemes**. 
- **Auto-Cleanup**: Automatically purges broken links (404) and expired schemes from the database.
- **Deep Sync**: Constantly monitors Ministry portals (AgriWelfare, myScheme, DBT) to ensure farmers never see outdated info.

### Predictive Forecasting
- **Smart Yield**: AI-driven models that predict crop success based on soil health and seasonal weather.
- **Market Intelligence**: Real-time price tracking and forecasting to help farmers sell at the peak.

### Multilingual Advisory Layer
- **AI Chatbot (Groq)**: Instant, expert answers to farming FAQs in Hindi, Odia, and English.
- **Voice Assistant**: Natural language speech-to-interpretation for hands-free advisory in the field.
- **Local Language Support**: Full "feature parity" across three primary languages to serve 100M+ farmers.
